How to order

01 /

Choose your dishes

Browse the current food menu in our Viber community and choose what you would enjoy.

02 /

Send a message or call

Order in the Viber group or by phone. Include your dishes, weights or portions, preferred date, delivery or pickup, and the delivery address.

03 /

Receive your order

Order a day ahead: place your order today and receive it the following day after 16:00. There is no Sunday delivery — agree the delivery date with us.

Delivery details

01 /

Weights & portions

Main dishes — from 500 g. Appetizers — from 300 g. For dishes sold by the portion, use the portion size and price shown in the current menu.

02 /

Prices & areas

Orders from UAH 1,200 — free delivery. Other orders — UAH 150. Sovinyon and Chornomorka — delivery is charged at the applicable rate regardless of order value. Kotovskyi residential area — delivery terms are agreed individually.

03 /

Something extra?

You can add frozen dishes such as varenyky, chebureky, meatballs, syrnyky and dumplings. Ask what is available at the cafe too — extra items can be added before delivery rounds begin.

04 /

Payment & receiving your order

Both the order and delivery can be paid by bank transfer. If the order arrives and you are not at the specified address, the delivery charge is paid by the customer.
